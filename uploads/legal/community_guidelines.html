Community Guidelines

Cocpit is built to empower professionals, creators, students, and employers to connect, collaborate, and grow together in a respectful, constructive environment. These Community Guidelines set the standards for acceptable behavior on Cocpit.

1. Be Professional and Authentic

  • Use your authentic professional identity and represent your experience accurately.
  • Maintain constructive, respectful interactions across posts, comments, messages, and project showcases.

2. Zero Tolerance Policy

  • No Harassment or Bullying: Personal attacks, targeted hate speech, intimidation, and discriminatory content will lead to immediate account suspension.
  • No Explicit Content: Sexually explicit material, graphic violence, or illegal activities are strictly forbidden.
  • No Spam or Phishing: Unsolicited promotional bulk spam, deceptive links, or financial scams are not permitted.

3. Respect Intellectual Property

Only share content, images, articles, and media that you own or have explicit authorization to publish.

4. Reporting and Moderation

If you encounter content or behavior that violates these guidelines, use the in-app Report button or contact support@cocpit.in. Our moderation team reviews reported violations promptly.
