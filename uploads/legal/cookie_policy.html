Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how Cocpit uses cookies and similar tracking technologies when you use our web platform and mobile applications.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your web browser or device when you visit a website. They help platforms recognize your session, remember your preferences, and provide a secure, seamless user experience.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential Cookies: Required for authentication, security, and session management.
  • Preferences & Settings Cookies: Remember your theme, language, and custom workspace layout preferences.
  • Analytics & Performance Cookies: Measure platform usage, page load times, and feature interaction to help us continuously optimize performance.

3. Managing Cookie Preferences

You can adjust or disable cookies at any time through your web browser settings. Note that disabling essential cookies may impact your ability to log in or use certain interactive features of Cocpit.

4. Contact Us

For questions about our cookie usage, reach out to support@cocpit.in.
