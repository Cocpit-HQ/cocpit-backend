Privacy Policy

At Cocpit ("we," "our," or "us"), we are committed to respecting your privacy and protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit or use the Cocpit platform.

1. Information We Collect

  • Personal Identity & Contact Information: Full name, account handle, email address, phone number, location, and credentials.
  • Professional Profile Data: Career history, educational background, skills, certifications, work preferences, portfolio links, and uploaded resumes/documents.
  • User Content: Posts, articles, comments, messages, media files, and interactive contributions shared on the platform.
  • Technical & Device Data: IP address, browser type, device identifier, operating system version, and system analytics.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the Cocpit networking and career platform.
  • To match candidates with relevant job opportunities and personalized career insights.
  • To calculate and present profile completeness scores to help strengthen professional presence.
  • To facilitate messaging, networking connections, and community engagement.
  • To enforce our Terms of Service, maintain security, and prevent fraudulent activity.

3. Data Sharing and Cloud Services

We do not sell your personal data. We share information only with essential infrastructure service providers under strict data protection agreements, or when legally required by law enforcement.

4. Data Retention & Account Deletion

You have full control over your personal data. You may request account deletion or export your data at any time via your Account Settings. Upon requested deletion, your personal records will be permanently removed from active databases in accordance with applicable legal retention requirements.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or privacy concerns regarding this policy, please contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@cocpit.in.
